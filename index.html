01 / WHO WE ARE

BUILT FOR
THE OPEN ROAD.

Sirski Transport is a friendly and professional Virtual Trucking Company built for drivers who share a passion for trucking, teamwork and the open road.

We operate European and US routes and bring our community together with a weekly convoy every Thursday throughout the year. Whether you are an experienced virtual trucker or looking for a welcoming VTC to grow with, there is a place for you here.
